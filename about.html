About Wellness Habit Tracker

Welcome to the Wellness Habit Tracker, your personal companion for building better habits and achieving your wellness goals. We believe that small, consistent actions lead to significant life changes.

Our Mission

Our mission is to empower individuals to take control of their daily routines and create lasting positive habits. Whether you're looking to drink more water, exercise regularly, meditate, or develop any other healthy habit, our tracker makes it simple and motivating.

Key Features

Track Your Habits

Easily add, manage, and track your daily habits with our intuitive interface. Mark habits as completed and watch your progress grow.

Visual Progress

Stay motivated with visual progress indicators and completion percentages that show how well you're doing at a glance.

Filter & Organize

Filter your habits by status (all, completed, or pending) to focus on what matters most to you each day.

Daily Inspiration

Get motivated with daily inspirational quotes that encourage you to keep going and stay committed to your wellness journey.

Dark Mode

Switch between light and dark themes for comfortable viewing at any time of day.

Why Habit Tracking Works

Research shows that tracking your habits increases accountability and awareness. By making your progress visible, you're more likely to stay consistent. Our tracker helps you:

  • Build awareness of your daily actions
  • Maintain motivation through visual progress
  • Develop consistency through regular check-ins
  • Celebrate small wins along the way
